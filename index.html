Our Story

Who we are

Baked with passion, finished with love

NIS Cakes & More is a cake and pastry hub based in Accra, Ghana. What started as a love for baking has grown into a brand known for fresh, beautiful cakes and pastries delivered right across the city — and to parts of Ghana beyond it.

Every order is made to order. We don't believe in cakes that sit on shelves — we believe in cakes baked for your moment, decorated for your theme, and delivered fresh for your celebration. From a child's first birthday to a grand wedding, we treat every cake like it matters, because to someone, it always does.

Our promise is simple, and it's right there in our name: one taste, and you'll be hooked. …addiction at first bite.
